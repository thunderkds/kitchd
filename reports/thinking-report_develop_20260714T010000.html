Problem: How should the theme-system work (ADR-0001, Option B) be decomposed into independently-gradable vertical-slice tasks for Stage 2?
Two tracer-bullet slices: T025 (backend theme-preference, C1/Low/P1, unblocked) → T026 (frontend token system + full migration + switcher UI, C2/Low/P1, blocked by T025).
The locked brainstorming constraints — account-synced persistence and full 17-file migration in one shot (no half-themed UI) — naturally produce exactly two end-to-end-verifiable slices rather than more. T026 cannot be split further (e.g. "tokens" vs "migration" vs "switcher UI") without violating the locked decision that the migration must land atomically, so a finer split was considered and explicitly rejected by the user during the granularity quiz.
